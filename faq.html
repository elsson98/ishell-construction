FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions about our services

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Ishell Construction offer?
We offer a comprehensive range of construction services including residential construction, commercial construction, renovations, and design & planning services. Our team can handle projects of various sizes, from small renovations to large commercial buildings.
How long has Ishell Construction been in business?
Ishell Construction has been building excellence since 2005. With over 20 years of experience in the construction industry, we have completed more than 500 successful projects throughout the UK.
How do I request a quote for my project?
You can request a quote by visiting our "Request Quote" page and filling out the form with details about your project. Alternatively, you can contact us directly by phone or email, and one of our representatives will get in touch with you to discuss your project requirements.
What areas do you serve?
We primarily serve London and the surrounding areas. However, we also take on projects throughout the UK depending on the size and scope of the work. Please contact us to discuss if we can service your location.
Do you handle permits and approvals?
Yes, we handle all necessary permits and approvals as part of our comprehensive service. Our team is familiar with local building codes and regulations, ensuring that your project complies with all requirements.
What is your typical project timeline?
Project timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of the work. A small renovation might take a few weeks, while a custom home could take several months. During the consultation, we'll provide you with a detailed timeline based on your specific project requirements.
Do you offer warranties on your work?
Yes, we stand behind our work. Ishell Construction offers warranties on all projects. The specific terms and duration of the warranty depend on the type of project and will be clearly outlined in your contract.
How do you handle changes to the project scope?
We understand that changes sometimes occur during construction. We have a formal change order process that documents any modifications to the original scope, including adjustments to the timeline and cost. This ensures transparency and clear communication throughout the project.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept multiple payment methods including bank transfers, credit cards, and checks. Payment schedules are typically structured with an initial deposit followed by installments based on project milestones.
Can I see examples of your previous work?
Yes, you can view our portfolio of completed projects on our "Previous Work" page. We also have detailed case studies available and can provide references from past clients upon request.
